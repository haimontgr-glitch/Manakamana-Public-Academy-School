About Us

Our Story

Manakamana Public Academy was established in 2071 B.S. in Kohalpur-2, Banke, with a vision to provide quality, accessible education to the local community. Since then, we have grown into a trusted institution known for academic excellence and holistic student development.

Our Mission

To nurture confident, disciplined, and knowledgeable students who are prepared to face future challenges with strong moral values and academic skills.

Our Vision

To be a leading educational institution in the region, recognized for innovation in teaching and commitment to student success.
